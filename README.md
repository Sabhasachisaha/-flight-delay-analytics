## Week 3 Findings — Window Functions & Final Analysis

Ranking carriers by average arrival delay confirms American Airlines (AA) as the worst performer at 15.31 minutes, while Republic Airways (YX) is the only carrier with a negative average (-1.79 minutes, arriving early). Delay trends vary sharply across the year: July averages 18.09 minutes of delay, while October drops to -1.28 minutes, a swing of over 19 minutes between the best and worst months. Breaking delay minutes down by cause shows carrier-related delay accounts for 58.1% of total delay minutes (35.8M), air traffic system (NAS) delay accounts for 31.8% (19.6M), and weather accounts for just 10.1% (6.2M) — confirming weather is a smaller factor than commonly assumed. At the route level, Hayden, CO to Boston (HDN-BOS) has the worst average delay at 117.97 minutes across 32 flights, though most of the worst-performing routes involve smaller regional airports with lower flight volume. Finally, on-time performance is highest on Tuesdays (81.4%) and lowest on Fridays (76.0%), consistent with Friday being one of the busiest travel days of the week.
