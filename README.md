## Week 2 Findings — SQL Analysis

Average arrival delay varies significantly by carrier, from American Airlines (AA) at 15.31 minutes (966K flights) to Republic Airways (YX) averaging -1.79 minutes early (295K flights). Delays peak in summer, with July averaging 18.09 minutes and only 71.13% on-time performance, compared to October's -1.28 minute average and 87.35% on-time rate. Late-aircraft delay (41.97M total minutes) and carrier delay (35.82M minutes) are the two largest contributors to arrival delays, far outweighing weather delay (6.2M minutes).
